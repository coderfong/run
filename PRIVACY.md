# Territory Run — Privacy Policy (stub)

_Last updated: 2026-07-05. This stub must be finalized and hosted at a
public URL (linked from the app's Profile screen and both store listings)
before submission._

## What we collect

- **Account**: your username and a salted hash of your password. No email,
  no phone number, no real name.
- **Routes**: the GPS trace of each run you record, but **only while a run
  is active**. We never track your location in the background outside an
  active run.
- **Territories**: the polygons derived from your closed loops, their area,
  and when they were captured.
- **Motion**: step counts from your device's pedometer during a run, used
  solely to verify runs are on foot (anti-cheat). Raw motion sensor data
  never leaves your device.
- **Diagnostics**: crash reports and performance telemetry (Sentry), only
  if enabled for the build you're running.

## What we do NOT do

- No ads, no sale of data, no third-party analytics beyond crash reporting.
- No background location tracking outside an active run.
- No contact list, camera, microphone, or photo access.

## Who can see what

- Your username, team, territory polygons and areas are **public** in-app
  (map and leaderboard) — claiming land is the game.
- Your raw GPS traces are not exposed via the public API.

## Deletion

Profile → Delete account permanently removes your account, runs, routes
and territories (hard delete with cascades). There is no retention period.

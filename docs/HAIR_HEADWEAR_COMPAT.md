# Hair + headwear compatibility

How a hairstyle and a headwear item combine on the PASER character, without
a hand-made image per pair. Written 2026-09-26.

## Where it lives

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `frontend/src/config/hairHeadwearCompat.json` | Hand-set table: families, every headwear id to a family, every hairstyle to a type and feature regions, pair overrides |
| `frontend/src/config/hatUndersides.json` | Generated by `scripts/measure-hat-underside.py`: every hat's lower edge, in its own art fractions |
| `frontend/src/config/headwearFit.js` | The rules: `getHeadwearFamily`, `getHairFeaturePolicy`, `getHairOcclusion`, `resolveHairClip` |
| `frontend/src/components/character/CharacterRig.js` | Draws the answer: the hair art cut by one even-odd SVG path |
| `frontend/src/config/hairUnderHat.json` | Unchanged: the Fit Studio's painted covers and with-hat hair positions |
| `frontend/scripts/hair-headwear-qa/` | `resolve.js` runs pairs through the app's own code; `render.py` draws them over the real art |

`CharacterRig` is the only place the character is composited: busts, the
home runner, feed, leaderboards, shop try-on, rivals, clubs, run screens,
share cards and the watch portrait (a capture of the rig) all go through it,
so they all get the same answer.

## The model

Draw order is unchanged: hat back half, body, head, face, **hair (clipped)**,
glasses, hat. The hair is clipped to what the hat leaves showing:

```
visible hair = hair art
             − painted cover (the scalp the hat hides; same for every style)
             − sky          (anything above the hat's own underside)
             + kept features (a bun through a cap, space buns beside a hat)
             − hidden features above the seat (no bun through a top hat)
```

Precedence, top wins:

1. A pair's own painted cover (`hairUnderHat.json cover[hat][hair]`) is the whole answer.
2. `COVERING` hair (the hijab) is never clipped. Every hat sits on top of it.
3. `FULL_ENCLOSURE` hides all hair.
4. A crown-`gathered` style (high bun) is hidden wherever its bun is hidden.
5. Otherwise: the hat's `'*'` cover (or the measured notch for a hat with no
   cover and no underside), plus the sky, minus kept features, plus hidden ones.

**Features** are the parts a scalp mask cannot judge: `topBun`, `sideBuns`
and `tail`. Each is a polygon in the hairstyle's own art fractions, so it
moves with the hair if the studio moves the hair under a hat. The family
sets each feature to `keep`, `hide` (removed above the hat's seat only, so a
tail still hangs out below a brim) or `cover`.

**The sky** is for crown-covering families. No hair may show above the
hat's underside in any column where the hat rests, meaning its underside is
within 0.12 head of its seat. That excludes horns, ears and pompoms. The sky
falls away at 60° past the brim's ends. The seat is recomputed from the
underside as drawn, because the stored `edgeY` goes stale when a hat is
moved.

## Overrides

Item-level overrides only; there are no pair overrides.

| Item | Override | Why |
|---|---|---|
| santahat | topBun hide, tail hide | The reference santa hides the bun; the beanie keeps it |
| headscarf | topBun, sideBuns, tail hide | Tied under the chin: nothing passes through it |
| ushanka, trapperhat, scrumcap, aviatorcap, kabuto, cur_desertflapcap, cur_trailflapcap | sideBuns hide | Ear flaps or side guards cover where space buns sit |

## After changing art or catalogue

```bash
python scripts/measure-hat-underside.py
```

Run that after any new or redrawn headwear art. New headwear or hairstyles
also need an entry in `hairHeadwearCompat.json`: `__tests__/headwearFit.test.js`
fails until they have one. Judge the result on renders, not numbers:

```bash
python scripts/hair-headwear-qa/render.py --reference              # the ten reference-sheet hats over every style
python scripts/hair-headwear-qa/render.py --hairs topknot,spacebuns # one style under every hat
python scripts/hair-headwear-qa/render.py --hats tophat --compare   # committed rules beside the working ones
```

## Known limits

- **Painted covers are data, and some are wrong for some styles.** The Viking
  helm's cover reaches below the helmet (a gap under it; roundfro and curls
  lose their sides). `cur_knitbeanie` and `dadcap` covers cut roundfro's and
  hs26's upper sides hard. The fix is to repaint them in the Fit Studio. The
  rules never override a painted cover.
- **Cut edges have no ink.** A cut through the hair art shows as a hard edge
  with no outline. That was already true of painted covers.
- **Gathered and updo styles have no "under a hat" art.** Under a structured
  hat or helmet, highbun disappears and hs25 keeps only a few curls. The
  reference sheets redraw these as pressed hair. That needs the hat-hair
  variant art (`scripts/hat-hair/`, `C:\Users\user\Desktop\hat-hair`); the
  rules would then pick the variant instead of hiding.
- The astronaut helmet's visor is opaque in the art, so the face does not show through it.
- The Fit Studio mirrors the family classification, the hijab, full
  enclosures and gathered styles. It does not preview features or the sky;
  use `render.py` for those.
